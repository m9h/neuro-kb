The [parameter lock](../student-materials/parameter-lock.json) records known ASCL1 identity and leaves unverified values blank. It is a planning record, not an executable culture SOP.

The project proceeds from method documentation to identity, function, interface validation, temporal-pattern classification and MNIST benchmarking. Controlled contingent adaptation follows as a separate extension. Negative results at any stage remain useful. No experimental dataset was supplied for this repository's initial article.

The initial article retains tissue engineering as a future extension. Planar-culture readiness and interpretable electrical measurements are the immediate milestones.
