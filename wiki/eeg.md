# Electroencephalography

Electroencephalography (EEG) records electric potential differences between electrodes on the scalp. The measurable signal arises from synchronous postsynaptic currents in cortical pyramidal neurons, whose apical dendrites are aligned roughly perpendicular to the cortical surface. Their aligned primary currents sum into equivalent current dipoles, and the return (volume) currents through the head's conductive tissues produce the scalp potentials. EEG is the electric counterpart of [MEG](meg.md). Both share the same neural generators and source-imaging machinery, but they differ in their sensitivity to source orientation and to skull conductivity.

## Physics

EEG operates in the **quasi-static regime**. Below roughly 1 kHz, electromagnetic wavelengths in tissue are far larger than the head, so capacitive, inductive and propagation effects are negligible (see [physics-electromagnetic.md](physics-electromagnetic.md)). The potential φ satisfies a Poisson equation with spatially varying conductivity σ:

```
∇ · (σ ∇φ) = ∇ · J_p        (J_p = primary neural current density)
n · (σ ∇φ) = 0  on the scalp (insulating boundary)
```

After discretising the source space and solving this equation for every source, the linear forward model is:

```
Y = L · J + n
```

The terms are:

- **Y** is the (channels × time) data.
- **L** is the leadfield computed with [BEM](method-bem.md) or [FEM](method-fem.md).
- **J** holds the source amplitudes.
- **n** is sensor noise.

This is the same equation that sits at the centre of the neurojax pipeline (`neurojax` README, Architecture).

Key physical consequences:

- **Orientation sensitivity.** EEG detects both radial and tangential dipoles. MEG is nearly blind to radial sources in a spherical head, which makes the two modalities complementary.
- **Reference dependence.** EEG measures potential *differences*, so every montage implies a reference. Common choices are linked mastoids, Cz, common average and REST. Source imaging must use the same reference in L and Y.
- **Skull smearing.** The resistive skull blurs and attenuates potentials. This is the dominant cause of EEG's 1-3 cm scalp resolution, and the main reason EEG forward models are more conductivity-sensitive than MEG models.

## Tissue Conductivities

Typical literature values used for EEG forward models are listed below. Full tables are on [tissue-electrical-properties.md](tissue-electrical-properties.md) and the individual tissue pages.

| Tissue | Conductivity (S/m) | Literature source | Wiki page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scalp | 0.43 | Gabriel et al. 1996 | [tissue-scalp.md](tissue-scalp.md) |
| Skull (compact + spongy, lumped) | 0.006-0.015 | Dannhauer et al. 2011 | [tissue-skull.md](tissue-skull.md) |
| CSF | 1.79 | Gabriel et al. 1996 | [tissue-csf.md](tissue-csf.md) |
| Gray matter | 0.33 | Gabriel et al. 1996 | [tissue-gray-matter.md](tissue-gray-matter.md) |
| White matter (isotropic approx.) | 0.14 | Gabriel et al. 1996 | [tissue-white-matter.md](tissue-white-matter.md) |

The brain-to-skull conductivity ratio is about **22-55×** (0.33/0.015 to 0.33/0.006). Uncertainty in skull σ is the largest single source of EEG forward-model error. CSF, the most conductive compartment at 1.79 S/m, shunts current tangentially and must be modelled explicitly in realistic head models. White matter is anisotropic, and neurojax derives conductivity anisotropy from DTI (`neurojax` README, Head Modeling).

neurojax also supports **qMRI-informed conductivity**. Its `sigma_from_qmri` function maps T1 and QMT bound-pool fraction (BPF) to σ differentiably, so gradients can flow back into tissue parameters.

## Signal Characteristics

| Parameter | Typical value |
|---|---|
| Scalp amplitude (raw, pre-amplification) | 10-100 µV (spontaneous); ERPs ~1-20 µV |
| Clinical bandwidth | 0.5-70/100 Hz |
| Research bandwidth | DC to several hundred Hz (high-gamma, HFOs) |
| Sampling rate | 250-5000 Hz |
| Channel counts | 19-21 (clinical 10-20 system); 32-128 (10-10); 256 (dense-array) |
| Spatial resolution | 1-3 cm (scalp topography) |
| Temporal resolution | ~1 ms |

### Frequency bands

The power spectrum follows an aperiodic 1/f^χ background with superimposed oscillatory peaks. The canonical bands are delta, theta, alpha, beta and gamma.

## Forward Modeling (Head Models)

The neurojax head-modeling pipeline runs in six steps (`neurojax` README):

1. **T1w image → CHARM segmentation.** CHARM produces a 60-tissue segmentation using SAMSEG plus an extended atlas.
2. **Surface extraction.** Tissue boundaries are extracted with marching cubes.
3. **Conductivity assignment.** Literature values are used, optionally refined with qMRI (σ(T1, BPF), DTI anisotropy).
4. **Solver.** A 3-layer BEM (MNE or OpenMEEG symmetric BEM) or FEM (JAX-FEM + PETSc) computes the fields.
5. **Leadfield L.** The solver output is assembled into the leadfield matrix.
6. **Inverse.** Any of the 15 inverse methods can then be applied.

The whole chain is differentiable:

```python
from neurojax.geometry.fem_forward import assemble_stiffness, sigma_from_qmri

sigma = sigma_from_qmri(t1_values, bpf_values, labels, params)  # differentiable
K = assemble_stiffness(vertices, elements, sigma)
grad_params = jax.grad(loss)(params)  # end-to-end through FEM
```

High-resolution reference anatomies for FEM-based EEG are [head-model-mida.md](head-model-mida.md) and [sci-head-model.md](sci-head-model.md). The SCI model is a tetrahedral head model built for EEG, EIT and transcranial ultrasound.

The method lineage behind this pipeline runs as follows:

- **CAUCHY/SimBio (1993+).** FEM forward modeling; its NeuroFEM kernel underlies BESA MRI and CURRY.
- **OpenMEEG.** Symmetric BEM with a subtraction approach for the dipole singularity.
- **Fijee (Cobigo 2014).** FEM forward modeling coupled with neural-mass dynamics for dense-array EEG source connectivity.

## Source Imaging (Inverse Problem)

Recovering J from Y is ill-posed because there are far more sources than channels. neurojax implements 15 solvers in five families (`neurojax:src/neurojax/source/`). Details are on [method-source-imaging.md](method-source-imaging.md).

| Family | Methods | Key idea |
|---|---|---|
| L2 minimum norm | MNE, dSPM, sLORETA, eLORETA | Tikhonov-regularised distributed estimates. dSPM and sLORETA add noise normalisation; eLORETA has zero localisation error for single sources in noise-free conditions. |
| Biophysical prior | LAURA, VARETA | LAURA uses a 1/d³ local autoregressive prior; VARETA uses adaptive spatial resolution. |
| Beamformers | LCMV, SAM, DICS | Data-adaptive spatial filters; DICS works in the frequency domain. |
| Bayesian sparse | CHAMPAGNE, HIGGS | CHAMPAGNE uses Type-II maximum likelihood (sparse Bayesian learning). HIGGS jointly estimates sources and connectivity via hidden Gaussian graphical spectral models. |
| Deep learning | PI-GNN | Physics-informed graph convolution on the cortical mesh. |

The project convention is that regularisation is always explicit and reported. It uses Tikhonov regularisation rather than truncated SVD with a hidden rank cutoff (`neurojax` CLAUDE.md):

```python
from neurojax.source import SourceGNN, mesh_to_graph, estimate_tikhonov_reg
graph = mesh_to_graph(vertices, faces)
reg = estimate_tikhonov_reg(leadfield)   # always explicit, always reported
```

The neurojax autoresearch loop benchmarks differentiable inverses against classical ones. Its metric is `source_advantage = (DLE_classical − DLE_neurojax) / DLE_classical`, where DLE is dipole localisation error. It is evaluated on simulated dipoles and real data (`neurojax:autoresearch/program.md`).

## Preprocessing

neurojax's `preprocessing/` module provides:

- ASR (artifact subspace reconstruction)
- ICA (FastICA and probabilistic ICA)
- Bandpass and notch filtering
- Channel interpolation
- Resampling

The IIR filter is a `jax.lax.scan` Direct-Form II Transposed implementation. It replaced a call to the non-existent `jax.scipy.signal.lfilter` (`neurojax:docs/BUG_REPORTS.md`, bug #5).

The `spatial/` module provides EEG channel graphs and spatial filters.

## Downstream Analysis

**Brain dynamics.** neurojax provides several state- and dynamics-inference methods:

- HMM and DyNeMo state inference on time-delay-embedded data; see [method-hmm-dynamics.md](method-hmm-dynamics.md).
- SINDy sparse system identification [@brunton2016discovering].
- Koopman / DMD operators [@schmid2010dynamic; @brunton2022modern].

**Covariance / Riemannian prediction.** coffeine builds sklearn-compatible pipelines on per-band covariance matrices [@sabbagh2020predictive]. The pipeline runs in three stages:

1. **Projection.** Spatial filtering mitigates field spread and handles rank deficiency, which arises for example after ICA or average referencing.
2. **Vectorisation.** Features are taken from variance, covariance, or the Riemannian tangent space.
3. **Learning.** A regression or classification model is fitted.

The Riemannian embedding is notable because it mitigates field spread and vectorises in a single step.

**Generative / biophysical models.**

- **Neural mass models.** vbjax simulates neural-mass networks whose outputs feed EEG forward models [@woodman2024insamuvbjax]; see [neural-mass-models.md](neural-mass-models.md) and [connectomics.md](connectomics.md).
- **Spectral fitting loop.** vbjax's autoresearch loop fits four model families to synthetic Liley EEG spectra using a log-spectral distance and BIC free energy for Bayesian model comparison (`vbjax:autoresearch/program.md`):
  - Liley (14 states, ground truth)
  - CMC (8 states)
  - RRW corticothalamic (8 states)
  - CBEI (8 states)
- **HNN dipoles.** hnn-jax reimplements the Human Neocortical Neurosolver dipole forward model differentiably, linking ERP components such as auditory P1/N1/P2 to circuit-level generators. Inference strategies are covered in [method-sbi.md](method-sbi.md).

**Foundation models.** See [method-eeg-foundation-models.md](method-eeg-foundation-models.md). nanoeeg pretrains under a fixed compute budget and publishes an identity-free score alongside its main score.

## Datasets

- **WAND (CUBRIC).** WAND has 170 subjects [@mcnabb2025wand]. Session ses-08 contains TMS-EEG (SICI protocol) stored as `.mat` files. neurojax fits these data in the `neurojax-tms/` derivatives (`empirical_tep.npy`, shape n_sensors × n_samples); see [tms.md](tms.md).
- **Wakeman-Henson.** A joint M/EEG dataset with a dedicated loader in `neurojax/io/`.
- **coffeine examples.** These use the MNE `kiloword` EEG dataset.
- **nanoeeg probe suite.** Uses 8 OpenEEGBench datasets plus three OpenNeuro datasets: `ds004504` (N=88, AD/FTD/HC), `ds006018` (N=127, ERP paradigms) and `ds005505` (N=136, HBN pediatric).

File formats (EDF/BDF, BrainVision, FIF, BIDS-EEG) are covered in [data-formats.md](data-formats.md). Electrode coordinate frames and coregistration are covered in [coordinate-systems.md](coordinate-systems.md).

## Relevant Projects

- **neurojax.** Differentiable EEG/MEG pipeline:
  - preprocessing (`preprocessing/`)
  - head modeling (`geometry/`)
  - 15 inverse solvers (`source/`)
  - dynamics (`models/`, `analysis/`)
  - TMS-EEG fitting on WAND
- **coffeine.** Covariance data frames and filter-bank Riemannian regression and classification for M/EEG prediction [@sabbagh2020predictive].
- **vbjax.** JAX virtual-brain neural-mass and neural-field simulation, which acts as the EEG generator. It includes a spectral-fitting autoresearch loop and reaches 26 Miter/s on a Quadro RTX 5000 [@woodman2024insamuvbjax].
- **hnn-jax.** A differentiable HNN dipole forward model checked against an `hnn_core` (NEURON) oracle:
  - **Fidelity.** The v1 population model reaches forward correlation +0.43 with the oracle, up from +0.22 for v0.
  - **Version pinning.** `hnn_core ≥ 0.6.0` is pinned because waveforms from versions 0.3 and 0.6.1 correlate at only 0.62.
- **nanoeeg.** A small EEG foundation model trained under a 1e18-FLOP budget (~1 H100-hour). It is evaluated on a frozen suite of 11 slots / 14 sub-probes scored with Cohen's κ plus balanced accuracy, and publishes an identity-free score to expose subject-identity leakage.
- **emeg-fm.** E/MEG foundation-model work; no source documents have been ingested yet.
- **zuna-eeg-pipeline.** EEG processing pipeline; no source documents have been ingested yet.
